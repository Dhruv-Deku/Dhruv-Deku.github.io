Will it work with my website?

Yes. If your site lets you add an embed code or a custom HTML block (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Shopify and most others do), it works. I can also host it on its own page and you link to it.

Do I own it?

Yes. Once it's paid for, the tool is yours. There's no monthly fee unless you choose the care plan.

What if my prices change?

Small price changes are free for the first 2 weeks. After that I can update them for you, or the care plan covers it.

Where do the leads go?

To your email by default. They can also go to a Google Sheet, your CRM, or a text message.

How do I pay?

50% to start and 50% once it's live, by card or bank transfer. If you prefer, we can work through Upwork.

    FAQ

    What does R-30 mean?

    R-value measures how well insulation resists heat flow. R-30 is a high rating, well suited to attics and ceilings in a hot climate.

    How many rolls do I need?

    Each roll covers 105 sq ft. Our calculator divides your square footage by that and rounds up to the next whole roll.

    Is the online price final?

    No. It's an estimate, confirmed by Efficient Pros after we review your property.

    Can I pay online?

    No. We follow up personally by WhatsApp or email to confirm the job, arrange payment and schedule installation.

    Do you offer contractor pricing?

    Yes, for licensed contractors ordering 12 rolls or more. Use the trade enquiry form above.
